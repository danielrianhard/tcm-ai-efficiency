Admission Criteria

Companies are out by default. Admission is an event, and it is rare. Strictness is the product: a small database of verified names is worth more than a large database of claims.

A — Plan

A highly specific, quantified and credible plan for driving AI efficiency into the business. Credible means it makes operational sense — not a promotional management team telling investors what they want to hear. Specific and quantified means named systems, named workflows, numeric targets and timelines. "We are leaning into AI" is nothing.

B — Realized

Satisfies A, and the benefit is already visible in the financial statements with the company quantifying it — the CHRW standard: shipments per person per day, quantified opex reduction attributed to named AI tooling.

What Counts as Evidence

A data point qualifies only if all four hold:

TestRequirement
It is a numberA percentage, dollar amount, ratio, headcount, throughput or unit-cost figure. Adjectives are not evidence.
It measures efficiencyCost, productivity per head, cycle time, unit economics, cost-driven margin — not revenue growth, and not revenue from selling AI products.
It is attributable to AIEither explicit company attribution, or a clean before/after around a named deployment with no better competing explanation.
It survives the noise checksSee the kill list below.

The Kill List

● The central problem
Nearly every company now talks about AI. Most efficiency gains attributed to AI are not caused by AI. The deep scan therefore runs two independent passes: one builds the case, a second tries to destroy it. A finding is admitted only if it survives the attempt to refute it.
Failure modeWhat it looks like
AI-washingGains actually driven by Lean, procurement, routing software, offshoring, layoffs or cycle recovery, relabelled as AI.
Promotional non-numbers"AI is transforming how we work." "Significant productivity gains." No figure, no evidence.
ImmaterialityA real number too small to matter — under roughly 0.5% of opex or operating income with no credible path to scale.
UnverifiableA number that appears once, is never reconciled to segment or financial data, and that management dodges on follow-up.
One-time vs run-rateA one-off saving presented as a recurring benefit. Always labelled.
Competing explanationVolume, price, mix, fuel or unrelated headcount cuts explain the delta. The peer set is checked: if the whole sub-industry posted the same move without an AI story, AI gets no credit.
AI as revenueSelling AI products is a different thesis. Logged, never scored.

Scoring

Conviction 0–100, starting at zero and awarded stingily.

ComponentMaxBasis
Quantification quality40How hard, specific and repeated the numbers are. Verbatim AI-attributed financial figures reconciled to the statements score high; one soft number scores low.
Financial-statement visibility25Whether the effect is visible in reported margin, opex and headcount trends independent of management's narrative.
Credibility of mechanism20Whether the causal story makes operational sense for this business model. Named systems, plausible arithmetic.
Track record15Whether management has hit prior quantified targets, and whether the same metric has held its definition across quarters.
Penalties—Promotional tone with thin numbers (−10 to −20); AI-revenue conflation (−10); metric definitions that shift quarter to quarter (−15).
Score stability
A score does not move without new evidence. There is no periodic re-scoring pass, because a number that drifts run to run cannot be used to track progress. Instead, the date a score last moved is displayed alongside it, and names whose evidence has gone three or more quarters without refresh are flagged stale rather than silently marked down.

▲ Why anchors matter
Every scan re-scores these three. If the anchors move, the run is treated as miscalibrated and is not written to the ledger. This is what keeps a score assigned in July comparable to one assigned in December.

Discipline Rules

The ledger is the only state. The site and any exported workbook are renderings of it.
The evidence log is append-only. History is never rewritten; corrections are new dated entries.
Every quantified claim carries its source — call date and document.
When evidence is borderline, the answer is no admission. Borderline names are recorded as near-misses and revisited next quarter.
A cut name is not re-litigated without new evidence that specifically overcomes the recorded cut reason.
If a transcript is not yet available for a company that has reported, its scan date is left unchanged so the next run retries it automatically.
