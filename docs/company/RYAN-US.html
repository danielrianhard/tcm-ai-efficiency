Investment Thesis

Ryan Specialty has genuinely quantified, explicitly AI-attributed OPERATIONAL proof points, but none of it is yet in the financials. On the Q1 2026 call (May 1, 2026) management cited a ~10x increase in facultative submissions per underwriter at Ryan Re (processing time cut from ~2 hours to minutes), an 11x uplift in submit-to-bind ratios and 36% faster median speed-to-quote at its Velocity property-cat MGU, and submission turnaround cut from ~24 hours to under 2 hours in Underwriting Management. However, these are narrow business-unit process metrics, not P&L impact: 2026 adjusted earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortisation and change in earn-outs (EBITDAC) margin is guided DOWN roughly 100-150 basis points, i.e. hundredths of a percentage point (bps), and the only quantified financial savings (~$80M annual run-rate from the 'Empower' program) do not begin until 2029 and are framed as automation/restructuring rather than cleanly AI-driven. This is a credible, specific plan with real operational evidence, but zero realized financial efficiency and margins currently moving the wrong way.

Key Risks & Red Flags

What would break this thesis
  • The impressive metrics (10x throughput, 11x bind ratio, 36% faster quotes) are cherry-picked, isolated business-unit process metrics (Ryan Re fac, Velocity cat MGU) that are unrepresentative of the ~$3B revenue base and have NOT been shown to translate into consolidated margin bps, G&A leverage, or headcount/volume decoupling.
  • AI is presently a NET COST: management explicitly guides 2026 margins DOWN ~100-150 bps and named AI/tech spend as a G&A headwind for multiple quarters.
  • The only quantified financial savings (~$80M/yr) come from the 'Empower' restructuring, which is ordinary automation/redundancy elimination plus M&A integration dressed with an AI narrative, and does not start hitting the P&L until 2029 - a distant, unverifiable target.
  • Much of the framing (Pat Ryan positioning RYAN as a 'net beneficiary' of AI, 'AI as an ally not adversary') is defensive narrative aimed at the disintermediation bear case rather than evidence of realized internal efficiency.
