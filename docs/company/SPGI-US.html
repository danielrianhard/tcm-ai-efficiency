Investment Thesis

S&P Global has genuine, quantified, explicitly AI/automation-attributed internal-efficiency datapoints, but they are small relative to the P&L and concentrated in only two of the last five quarters. The hard realized numbers come from the 2025 Enterprise Data Office (EDO) build-out: by Q4 2025 more than 50% of total data workflows were processed via automation tools, more than 10% of applications had been eliminated, and management cited $10M+ of research-productivity savings over the year (Q3 2025 added "multiple millions" from moving ~6,000 data operators onto an internally built content workflow tool). Management targets a >20% run-rate expense cut on the ~$0.5B EDO base (of a $7.5B cost base) by end-2027 and claims to be "well ahead of pace" — but that is a plan, not yet in the numbers. Crucially, the large margin expansion (52.1% adj. op margin, +330bps YoY in Q3 2025) is a multi-year operating-leverage/Ratings-cycle story management explicitly ties back to 2018, NOT cleanly AI-isolated, and by Q1 2026 the narrative had pivoted from internal efficiency to AI-as-revenue monetization. This is realized-but-entangled and immaterial, not elite.

Key Risks & Red Flags

What would break this thesis
  • Attribution ambiguity is the core problem: the impressive figure (52.1% margin, +330bps) is a multi-year operating-leverage and Ratings-cycle story management dates to 2018, not cleanly AI-isolated, while the cleanly AI-attributed savings ($10M+ research productivity, "multiple millions" EDO) are immaterial against a $7.5B cost base.
  • Confounds: With Intelligence M&A synergies and ordinary data-ops reorg/consolidation are bundled into the "automation" story.
  • Durability risk: hard efficiency numbers appear in only ~2 of 5 quarters (Q3/Q4 2025);
  • Q1 2025 had none and Q1 2026 pivoted to AI monetization, suggesting the internal-efficiency framing may be giving way to a promotional AI-revenue narrative.
  • The best quantified item — the >20% run-rate cut by 2027 — is a plan not yet visible in reported margins.
  • Added risks the deep-dive under-weights: (1) Disclosure discontinuity — with the Q1 2026 pivot to AI-as-revenue and a new CTO, management has stopped issuing fresh internal-efficiency datapoints; the 2027 >20% EDO run-rate target may never be re-quantified against actuals, making the plan hard to hold management to.
  • (2) Self-reported, unaudited magnitudes — the "multiple millions"/"multimillions" savings and the "$10M+" figure are management color, not separately disclosed line items; no external verification is possible.
  • (3) Attribution entanglement is structural — the headline +330bps margin expansion is primarily Ratings issuance-cycle and multi-year operating leverage; a debt-issuance downturn would compress margins regardless of AI, and could be spun either way.
  • (4) Denominator risk — even full success (~$100M) is ~1.3% of the cost base, so this thesis is essentially immaterial to consolidated EPS on its own.
