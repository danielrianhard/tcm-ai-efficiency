Investment Thesis

ADT has the most credible, durable *operational* AI story in consumer services: ~50% of service calls resolved remotely without truck rolls, sustained across at least four of the last five quarters, plus rising contact-center containment (chat 45%→60%, calls 16%→25% by April 2026) and over half of committed software code now AI-written with engineering headcount held flat. Every metric is specific, genuinely AI/automation-attributed, and repeated call-over-call. But the case fails the REALIZED bar: ADT has never disclosed an AI-attributed margin-basis points, i.e. hundredths of a percentage point (bps), selling, general and administrative expense (SG&A)-leverage, or headcount-decoupling figure in its actual financials. The hardest dollar figure is CFO Jeff Likosar's soft Q1 2026 range of "many millions, even tens of millions" in call-center/truck-roll savings, and management (Q3 2025) explicitly conceded benefits are still scaling and promised "more quantitative detail" later. This is an exceptional, well-quantified operational plan with early proof — not yet demonstrably in the numbers.

Key Risks & Red Flags

What would break this thesis
  • Attribution-to-financials gap is the core red flag: five quarters of strong operational metrics have never been converted into a disclosed, AI-attributed margin/SG&A/headcount number — the only dollar figure is the CFO's vague "tens of millions" range, and Q3 2025 management admitted savings are still scaling.
  • Confounds are material: ADT's P&L is dominated by RMR/subscriber economics, and the recent period featured a revenue miss and customer-retention headwinds, so any field-cost benefit is easily masked and unverifiable in reported financials.
  • Reversibility/deflection risk — "containment" can mean deferred rather than eliminated service demand.
  • Promotional risk is elevated because ADT's most heavily marketed AI (Origin AI, Gemini features) is a growth/revenue narrative, not internal efficiency, and could be conflated by investors.
  • Three risks the deep-dive understates: (1) Management's own Q3 2025 word was "plateaued" — remote-service resolution has been stuck at ~50% for several quarters, so the truck-roll lever may be near its ceiling, capping incremental savings.
  • (2) Denominator/definition risk: "100% of chats routed through AI" and "~half of calls routed through AI" describe routing, not resolution; actual containment is far lower (chat 45-60%, calls 16-25%), and these are self-reported, non-audited operational metrics whose definitions can drift quarter to quarter.
  • (3) Materiality: even "tens of millions" is small against ADT's ~$5B revenue and heavy interest burden, which is precisely why it has not shown up as a disclosed margin figure — the efficiency story is real but not yet financially load-bearing.
