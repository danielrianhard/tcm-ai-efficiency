Investment Thesis

Nu is a genuinely AI-first digital bank with a superb operating-leverage record (efficiency ratio 28.3% in Q2 2025 to 17.6% reported / 16.6% core in Q1 2026, cost-to-serve stable near $0.80), but only ONE quarter genuinely attributes operational efficiency to AI. In Q1 FY2026 (reported May 14, 2026) CFO Guilherme Lago said roughly 1/3 of the efficiency-ratio overperformance was "structural efficiency gains that are durable and compounding, mainly AI-driven improvements in operations and collections," with the other 2/3 explicitly non-structural timing that normalizes. The rest of the multi-year efficiency improvement is a scale/RPAC story management itself did not tie to AI, and in Q2-Q4 2025 AI was attributed to credit modeling/underwriting (revenue and risk quality, excluded here), with Q4 2025 even framing AI as a future cost pressure. Engineering throughput +50% year over year (YoY) and testing cycles 90% faster are real and AI-attributed but are development inputs, not P&L margin. A credible, well-articulated plan with a thin single-quarter realized signal, not durable realized AI-driven operating expense (opex) leverage.

Key Risks & Red Flags

What would break this thesis
  • Attribution is soft and single-quarter: the only AI-to-opex claim is a self-reported ~1/3 fraction of one quarter's efficiency beat with no mechanism-level breakdown, and 2/3 of that beat was explicitly non-structural timing.
  • The multi-year efficiency-ratio decline is a scale/operating-leverage confound (rising RPAC, fixed platform) that management did not attribute to AI until Q1 2026, and in Q2-Q4 2025 AI was tied to credit/underwriting (revenue and risk), not cost.
  • Reversibility/offset risk is high: FY2026 guidance is a roughly flat ~20% efficiency ratio because AI gains are explicitly offset by return-to-office and international-expansion costs, so the AI benefit will not be visible in net numbers this year.
  • Promotional risk exists given the heavily marketed "AI-first" framing (10x tokens, +50% throughput) around input metrics rather than proven P&L margin capture.
  • Beyond the thesis: (1) The realized AI-opex signal is thinner than "1/3" — Lago bundled "hiring discipline" and "software platform consolidation" into the same structural third and said AI was only "mainly" the operations/collections piece, so pure AI attribution is a sub-fraction of one third of one quarter's overperformance.
  • (2) Management's own IR release credits the efficiency beat to ARPAC/scale and flags AI as a forward COST (RTO + AI infrastructure), so the durable-compounding framing is a management assertion, not yet demonstrated across quarters — risk that Q2 2026 timing reversals mask or contradict it.
  • (3) No independent P&L line isolates AI savings; the dev-productivity metrics (throughput/tokens/testing) are unaudited inputs with no margin translation disclosed.
