Investment Thesis

Ingram Micro has delivered five consecutive quarters of operating-expense-leverage improvement that management explicitly and repeatedly attributes to "optimization and automation" from its Xvantage digital platform. CFO Michael Zilis quantified opex falling to 4.41% of net sales in Q4 FY2025 (a 74 bps YoY improvement from 5.15%) and 47 bps of FY2025 opex leverage, with 17-20 bps of YoY improvement in Q2/Q3 2025 and 12 bps of SG&A leverage in Q1 2026; CEO Paul Bay adds that in the lead Xvantage country "overall headcount has decreased" while revenue and gross profit per go-to-market head rose. The mechanism is genuine (touchless generative-AI "email-to-order," self-service, the IDA assistant lowering cost-to-serve). However, the AI-attributed portion is never isolated from ordinary "optimization," scale, and a one-off insurance recovery, so this is a realized-but-heavily-entangled case rather than a clean AI-efficiency proof.

Key Risks & Red Flags

What would break this thesis
  • Attribution is the core weakness: management always bundles "optimization AND automation," never isolating the AI-specific bps, so an unknown share is ordinary cost discipline and recurring restructuring charges.
  • The cleanest figure (Q4's 74 bps) is explicitly polluted by an insurance recovery and offsetting reserves, making it non-repeatable.
  • The bigger confound is scale — net sales grew 7-14% YoY every quarter while opex dollars also rose (Q1'26 opex +$75M/+12%), so much of the falling opex ratio is a denominator/operating-leverage effect from volume, not AI.
  • Finally, most of INGM's headline "AI" (AI-led net sales +60%, AI-infrastructure deal mix, $1B+ email-to-order GMV) is a revenue story, not internal efficiency, and the genuine efficiency claims (headcount decoupling) remain unquantified.
  • The Q4 FY2025 74 bps opex improvement — the largest single data point — is explicitly inflated by a one-off insurance recovery tied to the July 2025 ransomware incident, with no disclosed split between the AI/automation benefit and the one-off; taken at face value it overstates the run-rate AI efficiency.
  • Separately, opex-as-a-percent-of-sales declines partly as an arithmetic consequence of 8-14% net-sales growth (operating leverage on a rising denominator), so an unquantified but likely material portion of every quarter's 'leverage' is scale and mix, not AI. 'Optimization and automation' also bundles conventional headcount restructuring with Xvantage automation, and Xvantage is a broad digital/e-commerce platform of which only part is genuinely AI.
